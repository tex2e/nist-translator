Abstract. Recently proposed quantum key distribution protocols are shown to be vulnerable to a classic man-in-the-middle attack using entangled pairs created by Eve. It appears that the attack could be applied to any protocol that relies on manipulation and return of entangled qubits to create a shared key. The protocols that are cryptanalyzed in this paper were proven secure with respect to some eavesdropping approaches, and results reported here do not invalidate these proofs. Rather, they suggest that quantum cryptographic protocols, like conventional protocols, may be vulnerable to methods of attack that were not envisaged by their designers.

Introduction. The history of cryptography is replete with examples of protocols that were believed to be secure but shown to be vulnerable to novel attacks, often years after their design. Although sophisticated tools (and even specialized logics) have been designed to analyze and prove various properties of protocols, it is generally accepted that the best assurance of security is obtained through careful review by many experts familiar with vulnerabilities of similar protocols. An experience base of known vulnerabilities is a crucial component of this review. Because it is so new that there were no commercial products available before this year, little is known of vulnerabilities that may occur in the design and implementations of quantum cryptographic protocols. But if the history of cryptography is a reliable guide, it should be expected that even quantum cryptographic protocols designed by experts will have unanticipated vulnerabilities. The results presented in this paper suggest that this expectation is as true for quantum cryptography as it is for conventional.

The attack requires Eve to know or guess the basis that is used by Alice and Bob, but since no classical communication is exchanged, the basis must be the same throughout the protocol. In a realistic implementation, the basis will be either standard, or chosen from a small number of possibilities that Eve can guess and determine quickly in a high traffic network. Since any realistic implementation will have less than perfect transmission, Eve can evade detection by removing qubits at a low enough rate to remain below the normal transmission error rate. Eve can measure random qubits in a basis of her choosing, obtaining a long-term distribution of values for 0 and 1 in her basis, designated \(P_0 = a^2\) and \(P_1 = \beta^2\) for the proportion of 0 and 1 respectively. If she has guessed correctly, she will obtain \(P_0 = P_1 = .50\). If not, she simply rotates her basis enough to obtain the 50/50 distribution, an angle of \(\theta = (\cos^{-1}\sqrt{P_0}) - \pi/4\). This relationship can be seen from Figure 1.

Eve must have the ability to intercept the control (eavesdropping detection) bits sent from Bob to Alice, measure the received qubit and substitute a value to be returned to Alice in real time. Compared with attacks on classical cryptographic protocols, these assumptions are fairly minimal, and not at all unrealistic. The examples presented in this section illustrate that quantum cryptographic protocols may be vulnerable to many of the same attacks as classical protocols. Furthermore, as with conventional protocols, quantum protocols may be vulnerable due to subtle design issues. A protocol that is similar to ping-pong coding, developed by Beige, Englert, Kurtsiefer, and Weinfurter [3], is not vulnerable to the EPR man-in-the-middle attack because the location of the control bits is not known until completion of qubit transmission. Therefore Eve could not determine which bits to measure and what value to substitute and return to Alice.

原本 PDF p.4

Eve には、Bob から Alice に送られる制御（盗聴検知）ビットを傍受し、受け取った量子ビットを測定し、Alice に返す値をリアルタイムで置き換える能力が必要です。古典暗号プロトコルへの攻撃と比べると、これらの仮定はかなり控えめで、決して非現実的ではありません。この節で示した例は、量子暗号プロトコルが、古典プロトコルと同じ多くの攻撃に対して脆弱である可能性があることを示しています。さらに、従来のプロトコルと同様に、量子プロトコルは微妙な設計上の問題によって脆弱になる可能性があります。Beige、Englert、Kurtsiefer、Weinfurter [3] が開発した、ピンポン符号化に類似したプロトコルは、制御ビットの位置が量子ビットの送信の完了まで知られないため、EPR 中間者攻撃に対して脆弱ではありません。したがって、Eve は、どのビットを測定し、どの値に置き換えて Alice に返せばよいかを判定できませんでした。

A protocol developed by Cai [4] uses the ping-pong protocol without a public channel. The Cai protocol attempts to add security by randomizing the Bell state of the receiving party, rather than using a consistent state as in Bostrom and Feilberger. To transmit a bit, Alice selects at random from one of two Bell states: \(\Psi^+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle + \lvert 10\rangle)\) or \(\Phi^+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\). The protocol proceeds as follows:

原本 PDF p.4

Cai [4] が開発したプロトコルは、公開チャネルなしでピンポンプロトコルを使います。Cai のプロトコルは、Bostrom と Feilberger のように一貫した状態を使うのではなく、受信側のベル状態をランダム化することで、安全性を高めようとしています。ビットを送信するために、Alice は 2 つのベル状態 \(\Psi^+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle + \lvert 10\rangle)\) または \(\Phi^+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\) のうち 1 つをランダムに選びます。プロトコルは次のように進みます。

1.Alice prepares two qubits in either of the two states \(\Psi^+\) or \(\Phi^+\), selected randomly.

原本 PDF p.5

1.Alice は、ランダムに選んだ 2 つの状態 \(\Psi^+\) または \(\Phi^+\) のいずれかで 2 つの量子ビットを準備します。

2.Alice sends one of the qubits from the EPR pair to Bob and retains the other.

原本 PDF p.5

2.Alice は、EPR 対の量子ビットの一方を Bob に送り、もう一方を保持します。

3.To encode a ‘1’, Bob uses the operator \(\sigma_z\) on the received qubit; to encode ‘0’, he does nothing to the received qubit.

原本 PDF p.5

3.‘1’ を符号化するには、Bob は受け取った量子ビットに演算子 \(\sigma_z\) を作用させ、‘0’ を符号化するには、受け取った量子ビットに何もしません。

4.Bob returns the qubit to Alice.

原本 PDF p.5

4.Bob は量子ビットを Alice に返します。

5.Alice receives the travel qubit, combines it with the qubit she retained, and does a Bell measurement on the pair. If the state of the EPR pair created in Step 1 was \(\Psi^+\) then the measurement is then decoded as:
\(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle + \lvert 10\rangle) = 0\); \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle - \lvert 10\rangle) = 1\). If the result of the Bell measurement is \(\Phi^+\) or \(\Phi^-\), then Eve is detected.
If the EPR pair state was \(\Phi^+\), then the decoding step is
\(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle) = 0\); \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle - \lvert 11\rangle) = 1\). If the result of the Bell measurement is \(\Psi^+\) or \(\Psi^-\), then Eve is detected.

原本 PDF p.5

5.Alice はトラベル量子ビットを受け取り、保持している量子ビットと組み合わせ、その対に対してベル測定を行います。手順 1 で作成した EPR 対の状態が \(\Psi^+\) だった場合、測定結果は次のように復号されます。
\(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle + \lvert 10\rangle) = 0\); \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 01\rangle - \lvert 10\rangle) = 1\)。ベル測定の結果が \(\Phi^+\) または \(\Phi^-\) である場合、Eve が検知されます。
EPR 対の状態が \(\Phi^+\) だった場合、復号の手順は次のとおりです。
\(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle) = 0\); \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle - \lvert 11\rangle) = 1\)。ベル測定の結果が \(\Psi^+\) または \(\Psi^-\) である場合、Eve が検知されます。

Like the protocols described previously, Eve can obtain a perfect copy of the message transmitted from Bob to Alice. The attack is essentially the same:

原本 PDF p.5

前に説明したプロトコルと同様に、Eve は Bob から Alice に送信されたメッセージの完全な写しを得られます。攻撃は基本的に同じです。

•At step 2, Eve captures the qubit, then forwards to Bob one qubit of a two-qubit system that she has created in EPR state \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\).

原本 PDF p.5

•手順 2 で、Eve は量子ビットを捕捉し、EPR 状態 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\) で自分が作成した 2 量子ビットの系のうち 1 つの量子ビットを Bob に転送します。

•At step 3, Bob encodes ‘0’ or ‘1’ using \(\sigma_z\) then returns the qubit to Eve, thinking it is being sent to Alice. Eve does a Bell state measurement to decode the received qubit: state \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\) is decoded as ‘0’; state \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle - \lvert 11\rangle)\) is decoded as ‘1’. She executes either \(\sigma_z\) (for ‘1’) or does nothing (for ‘0’) to the captured qubit to encode the bit sent by Bob, then forwards it to Alice.

原本 PDF p.5

•手順 3 で、Bob は \(\sigma_z\) を用いて ‘0’ または ‘1’ を符号化し、Alice に送っていると思い込んで量子ビットを Eve に返します。Eve は、ベル状態測定を行って、受け取った量子ビットを復号します。状態 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle + \lvert 11\rangle)\) は ‘0’ として、状態 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\lvert 00\rangle - \lvert 11\rangle)\) は ‘1’ として復号されます。Eve は、Bob が送ったビットを符号化するために、捕捉した量子ビットに \(\sigma_z\) を作用させる（‘1’ の場合）か何もしない（‘0’ の場合）かを行い、それを Alice に転送します。

Although the Cai protocol appears at first to be secure because the Bell state is chosen randomly in Step 1, this attack needs fewer assumptions to succeed than the attack on the ping-pong protocol. Eve needs only the ability to intercept qubits and replace them with hers, and to determine the basis used by Alice, which can be done using a relatively small number of intercepted qubits using the procedure described earlier.

原本 PDF p.5

Cai のプロトコルは、手順 1 でベル状態がランダムに選ばれるため、一見安全に見えますが、この攻撃は、ピンポンプロトコルへの攻撃よりも少ない仮定で成功します。Eve に必要なのは、量子ビットを傍受して自分のものと置き換える能力と、Alice が使う基底を判定する能力だけです。基底の判定は、前述の手順を用いて、比較的少数の傍受した量子ビットで行えます。

Discussion and Conclusions. The EPR man-in-the-middle attack works because Alice and Bob cannot detect that qubits received by Bob are part of an entangled pair sent by Eve. Although the two protocols use different sigma operators (X and Z respectively), Eve can duplicate the actions of Bob without detection, regardless of the operator used by Bob. In the first case, this occurred because of the lack of conventional communication, and in the second because Eve was assumed able to control the classical communication between Alice and Bob, and the protocol did not provide steps for them to authenticate messages. Adapting methods described by Vaidman et al. [5], it would always be possible for Eve to distinguish the sigma operator applied by Bob. It thus appears that any protocol similar to the ones described above would be vulnerable.

原本 PDF p.5

考察と結論。 EPR 中間者攻撃が成立するのは、Bob が受け取った量子ビットが Eve の送ったエンタングルした対の一部であることを、Alice と Bob が検知できないためです。2 つのプロトコルは異なるシグマ演算子（それぞれ X と Z）を使いますが、Eve は、Bob が使う演算子にかかわらず、検知されることなく Bob の動作を複製できます。1 つ目の場合は従来の通信方式がないために、2 つ目の場合は Eve が Alice と Bob の間の古典通信を制御できると仮定されており、プロトコルにメッセージを認証する手順が備わっていなかったために、これが起きました。Vaidman ら [5] が説明した方法を応用すれば、Eve が Bob の作用させたシグマ演算子を常に区別することが可能になります。したがって、上述のプロトコルに類似するプロトコルは、いずれも脆弱であると考えられます。
