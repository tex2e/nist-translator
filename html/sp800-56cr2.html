1 Introduction

原本 PDF p.9

1 はじめに

未レビュー訳

During the execution of a public key-based key-establishment scheme specified in either of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publications [SP 800-56A]1 or [SP 800-56B],2 a key-derivation method may be required to obtain secret cryptographic keying material. This Recommendation specifies the key-derivation methods that can be used, as needed, in those key-establishment schemes. The keying material derived using these methods shall be computed in its entirety before outputting any portion of it and shall only be used as secret keying material, such as a symmetric key used for data encryption or message integrity, a secret initialization vector, or, perhaps, a key-derivation key that will be used to generate additional keying material (possibly using a different derivation process; see [SP 800-108]3). The derived keying material shall not be used as a key stream for a stream cipher.

原本 PDF p.9

国立標準技術研究所（NIST）の特別刊行物[SP 800-56A]1または[SP 800-56B]2に規定される公開鍵ベースの鍵確立方式の実行中に、秘密の暗号鍵素材を得るため鍵導出方法が必要となる場合がある。本推奨事項は、これらの鍵確立方式で必要に応じて使用できる鍵導出方法を規定する。これらの方法で導出する鍵素材は、その一部を出力する前に全体を計算しなければならず、データ暗号化またはメッセージ完全性のための対称鍵、秘密の初期化ベクトル、あるいは追加の鍵素材（別の導出処理を使用する場合もある。 [SP 800-108]3参照）の生成に用いる鍵導出鍵など、秘密の鍵素材としてのみ使用しなければならない。導出した鍵素材をストリーム暗号のキーストリームとして使用してはならない。

未レビュー訳

This Recommendation specifies two categories of key-derivation methods that can be employed, as required, to derive keying material from a shared secret Z generated during the execution of a key-establishment scheme specified in [SP 800-56A] or [SP 800-56B].

原本 PDF p.10

本推奨事項は、[SP 800-56A]または[SP 800-56B]に規定された鍵確立方式の実行中に生成される共有秘密Zから、必要に応じて鍵素材を導出するために使用できる2種類の鍵導出方法を規定する。

未レビュー訳

In addition to the currently approved techniques for the generation of the shared secret Z as specified in SP 800-56A and SP 800-56B, this Recommendation permits the use of a “hybrid” shared secret of the form Z′= Z || T, a concatenation consisting of a “standard” shared secret Z that was generated during the execution of a key-establishment scheme (as currently specified in [SP 800-56A] or [SP 800-56B]) followed by an auxiliary shared secret T that has been generated using some other method. The content, format, length, and method used to generate T must be known and agreed upon by all parties that will rely upon the derived keying material, as well as by any agents trusted to act on their behalf. The key-derivation methods specified in this Recommendation will process a hybrid Z′in the same way they process a standard Z. Therefore, for simplicity of notation and exposition, any shared secret denoted by the symbol Z in the remainder of this Recommendation can be of either the “standard” or “hybrid” variety.

原本 PDF p.10

[SP 800-56A]または[SP 800-56B]に規定された共有秘密Zの生成に関する現行の承認済み技法に加え、本推奨事項では、Z′= Z || Tという形式の「ハイブリッド」共有秘密の使用を認める。これは、鍵確立方式の実行中に生成された「標準」共有秘密Z（現行の[SP 800-56A]または[SP 800-56B]の規定による）に、別の方法で生成された補助共有秘密Tを連結したものである。導出した鍵素材に依存するすべての当事者、およびその代理として行動することを信頼されたエージェントは、Tの内容、形式、長さ、生成方法を認識し、合意していなければならない。本推奨事項の鍵導出方法は、標準Zと同じ方法でハイブリッドZ′を処理する。したがって、記法と説明を簡潔にするため、本推奨事項の以降でZと表記する共有秘密は、「標準」型または「ハイブリッド」型のいずれでもよい。

The first category of specified key-derivation methods consists of a family of one-step key-derivation functions that derive keying material of a desired length from a shared secret that was generated during the execution of a key-establishment scheme (and possibly other information as well).

Since NIST’s [SP 800-108] specifies several families of key-derivation functions that are approved for deriving additional keying material from a given cryptographic key-derivation key, those functions are employed in the second (key-expansion) step of these two-step procedures.

8.1 Using a Truncated Hash Function

原本 PDF p.36

8.1 切り詰めハッシュ関数の使用

未レビュー訳

SHA-224, SHA-512/224, SHA-512/256, and SHA-384 are among the approved hash functions specified in [FIPS 180]. 8.4 Destruction of Sensitive Locally Stored Data

原本 PDF p.37

K

未レビュー訳

Good security practice dictates that implementations of key-derivation methods include steps that destroy potentially sensitive locally stored data that is created (and/or copied for use) during the execution of a particular process; there is no need to retain such data after the process has been completed. Examples of potentially sensitive locally stored data include local copies of shared secrets that are employed during the execution of a particular process, intermediate results produced during computations, and locally stored duplicates of values that are ultimately output by the process. The destruction of such locally stored data ideally occurs prior to or during any exit from the process. This is intended to limit opportunities for unauthorized access to sensitive information that might compromise a key-establishment transaction.
