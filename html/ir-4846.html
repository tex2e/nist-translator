序文

This National Institute of Standards and Technology Interagency Report (NISTIR) is a compendium of computer security training and awareness courses. The purpose of this publication is to assist federal agencies locate computer security training resources. This publication is part of a continuing NIST effort to support federal agencies in accordance with NIST’s mandate under the Computer Security Act of 1987. These courses are organized into training areas within audience categories as defined in NIST Special Publication 500-172, Computer Security Training Guidelines.

原本 PDF p.5

この国立標準技術研究所省庁間報告書（NISTIR）は、コンピュータセキュリティの研修・意識向上コースの概要集です。本書の目的は、連邦機関がコンピュータセキュリティの研修資源を探す助けとなることです。本書は、1987年コンピュータセキュリティ法（Computer Security Act of 1987）に基づく NIST の責務に沿って連邦機関を支援する、NIST の継続的な取り組みの一部です。これらのコースは、NIST Special Publication 500-172, Computer Security Training Guidelines で定義された対象者区分ごとに、研修分野へ整理されています。

NIST Special Publication 500-172 was developed to provide a framework for identifying computer security training requirements for a diversity of audiences. It focuses on learning objectives based upon the extent to which computer security knowledge is required by an individual as it applies to his or her job function.

原本 PDF p.5

NIST Special Publication 500-172 は、多様な対象者に対するコンピュータセキュリティ研修の要件を特定するための枠組みを提供するために作成されました。この文書は、個人の職務に応じて、その人にどの程度のコンピュータセキュリティの知識が必要かに基づく学習目標に重点を置いています。

A training matrix was introduced in Special Publication 500-172 to assist agencies in designing training that meets the learning objectives for a particular group. The training and awareness courses in this compendium have been mapped to the matrix (see appendix A) by the training organization/vendor.

原本 PDF p.5

Special Publication 500-172 では、特定のグループの学習目標を満たす研修を機関が設計する際の助けとして、研修マトリクスが導入されました。本概要集の研修・意識向上コースは、研修機関・ベンダーによってこのマトリクスに対応付けられています（付録 A を参照）。

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) makes no claim or endorsement of the computer security courses or their currency in this compendium. Courses listed in the compendium were sent in as a result of a public data call during the 1991 calendar year. The data call consisted of a letter of invitation to known sources of trainers as provided by the Federal Computer Security Program Managers’ Forum; and the Federal Information Systems Security Educators’ Association. In addition, a CSL Newsletter was disseminated nationwide inviting vendors to participate in this effort. Therefore, this listing is not a complete source of all available security related courses.

原本 PDF p.5

国立標準技術研究所（NIST）は、本概要集に掲載されたコンピュータセキュリティコースやその最新性について、いかなる保証も推奨も行いません。掲載されたコースは、1991年の暦年中に行われた公開データ募集の結果として寄せられたものです。このデータ募集は、Federal Computer Security Program Managers’ Forum および Federal Information Systems Security Educators’ Association が提供した、研修担当者の既知の情報源への招待状で構成されていました。加えて、ベンダーにこの取り組みへの参加を呼びかける CSL Newsletter が全国に配布されました。したがって、この一覧は、利用可能なすべてのセキュリティ関連コースを網羅した情報源ではありません。

Vendors already listed are encouraged to continue to send in changes/updates and new vendors are invited to send in their computer security courses. (See address below.) NIST expresses its appreciation to the many federal, academic, and vendor organizations that participated in this effort for their time and interest in mapping their courses to the matrix in NIST Special Publication 500-172.

11. ABSTRACT (A 200-WORD OR LESS FACTUAL SUMMARY OF MOST SIGNIFICANT INFORMATION. IF DOCUMENT INCLUDES A SIGNIFICANT BIBLIOGRAPHY OR LITERATURE SURVEY, MENTION IT HERE.)The training and awareness courses in this compendium correspond to the matrix in NIST Special Publication 500-172, Computer Security Training Guidelines. Special Publication 500-172 is used as reference under the OPM regulation that implements Public Law 100-235, the Computer Security Act of 1987, which requires training for all employees responsible for the management and use of federal computer systems that process sensitive information. Under the regulation, agencies will be responsible for identifying the employees to be trained and providing appropriate training.
This publication is divided into five audience categories: 1) Executives, 2) Program/Functional Managers, 3) IRM, Security, and Audit Personnel, 4) ADP Management, Operations, and Programming Staff, and 5) End Users. In addition to the five audience categories, there are five training content areas for each audience category: 1) Computer Security Basics, 2) Security Planning & Management, 3) Computer Security Policy & Procedures, 4) Contingency Planning, and 5) Systems Life Cycle Management. The level of training required in each area will vary from general awareness to specific training courses depending on the training objectives established by each agency.
