Abstract. Mars, RC6, Rijndael, Serpent and Twofish were selected as finalists for the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES). To evaluate the finalists’ suitability as random number generators, empirical statistical testing is commonly employed. Although it widely believed that these five algorithms are indeed random, randomness testing was conducted to show that there is empirical evidence supporting this belief. In this paper, NIST reports on the studies that were conducted on the finalists for the 192-bit key size and 256-bit key size. The results to date suggest that all five of the finalists appear to be random.

概要。Mars、RC6、Rijndael、Serpent、Twofish が、高度暗号化標準（AES）の最終候補に選ばれました。最終候補が乱数生成器として適しているかどうかを評価するため、経験的な統計的検定が一般に用いられます。これら 5 つのアルゴリズムは実際にランダムであると広く信じられていますが、この信念を裏付ける経験的な証拠があることを示すために、ランダムネス検定を実施しました。本稿では、NIST が、192 ビット鍵長と 256 ビット鍵長について最終候補に対して実施した研究を報告します。現時点までの結果は、5 つの最終候補すべてがランダムであるように見えることを示唆しています。

Randomness testing was performed using the following strategy:

原本 PDF p.3

ランダムネス検定は、次の方針で実施しました。

(a) Input parameters such as the sequence length, sample size, and significance level5 were fixed for each sample6. These parameters were set at \(2^{20}\) bits, 300 binary sequences, and 0.01, respectively. For each binary sequence and each statistical test, a P-value was reported.

原本 PDF p.3

(a) 系列長、標本サイズ、有意水準5などの入力パラメータは、各標本6について固定しました。これらのパラメータは、それぞれ \(2^{20}\) ビット、300 個の 2 値系列、0.01 に設定しました。各 2 値系列と各統計的検定について、P 値が報告されました。

(b) For each P-value, a success/failure assessment was made based on whether or not it exceeded or fell below the pre-selected significance level.

原本 PDF p.3

(b) 各 P 値について、あらかじめ選んだ有意水準を上回ったか下回ったかに基づいて、成功・失敗の評価を行いました。

(c) For each statistical test and each sample, two evaluations were made. First, the proportion of binary sequences in a sample that passed the statistical test was calculated. The P-value for this proportion is equal to the probability of observing a value equal to or greater than the calculated proportion. Second, an additional P-value was calculated, based on a \(\chi^2\) test (with nine degrees of freedom) applied to the P-values in the entire sample to ensure uniformity.

原本 PDF p.3

(c) 各統計的検定と各標本について、2 つの評価を行いました。第 1 に、標本の中で統計的検定に合格した 2 値系列の割合を計算しました。この割合に対する P 値は、計算された割合以上の値が観測される確率に等しくなります。第 2 に、標本全体の P 値に自由度 9 の \(\chi^2\) 検定を適用して、一様性を確認するための追加の P 値を計算しました。

(d) For both measures described in step (c), an assessment was made. A sample was considered to have passed a statistical test if it satisfied both the proportion and uniformity assessments. If either of the two P-values in step (c) fell below 0.0001, then the sample was flagged as suspect7. If this occurred, additional samples were evaluated. Otherwise, the sample was believed to have satisfied the criterion for being random (from the point of view of a specific statistical test).

原本 PDF p.3

(d) 手順 (c) で述べた 2 つの尺度の両方について、評価を行いました。標本は、割合と一様性の両方の評価を満たした場合に、統計的検定に合格したと見なしました。手順 (c) の 2 つの P 値のいずれかが 0.0001 を下回った場合、その標本は疑わしいもの7として印を付けました。そのような場合には、追加の標本を評価しました。そうでなければ、その標本は（特定の統計的検定の観点から）ランダムであるための基準を満たしたものと見なしました。

3.2 Empirical Results & Analysis

原本 PDF p.4

3.2 経験的な結果と分析

The application of the aperiodic template11 statistical test on Serpent’s Plaintext/Ciphertext Correlation (based on 256-bit keys) yielded 12 rejections out of a sample of 300 binary sequences. According to the probability table in Appendix C, the P-value of such an event is 0.0000629219.

原本 PDF p.4

Serpent の Plaintext/Ciphertext Correlation（256 ビット鍵に基づく）に非周期テンプレート11の統計的検定を適用したところ、300 個の 2 値系列の標本のうち 12 個が棄却されました。付録 C の確率表によれば、このような事象の P 値は 0.0000629219 です。

Subsequent experiments were conducted for this particular case. In all, a total of 1,167,600 binary sequences were evaluated. This constituted 3,892 repetitions of the original experiment. In no other instance did such an event occur. Therefore, the 12 rejections obtained from the original experiment could be considered as an anomaly. If the 3,892 samples were collectively viewed as a single data set, the resulting number of rejections was 11,758, a number well within acceptable limits (i.e., the ideal number of rejections would be 11,676).

原本 PDF p.4

この特定のケースについて、追加の実験を実施しました。合計で 1,167,600 個の 2 値系列を評価しました。これは、元の実験を 3,892 回繰り返したことに相当します。このような事象が起きたケースは、ほかにはありませんでした。したがって、元の実験で得られた 12 個の棄却は、異常値と見なせます。3,892 個の標本を全体として 1 つのデータセットと見なした場合、棄却の数は 11,758 であり、許容範囲内に十分収まる数でした（すなわち、棄却の理想的な数は 11,676 です）。

There were a few issues to address in defining partial round versions of the algorithms. In fact, the outputs of the final rounds for the algorithms were not considered at all, because full round testing was previously conducted for low-density plaintexts under 128-bit keys during AES Round 1 analysis. Subkeys were applied in their original order, as were rounds, so that if a modified final round was prescribed (Rijndael, Serpent), then it was never used.

5 まとめと結論

During the AES Round 2 analysis period, NIST further investigated the randomness of the output for each of the finalists. The range of analyses was extended beyond the 128-bit keysize case conducted during the AES Round 1 analysis period. In this study, empirical statistical testing was performed on the 192-bit and 256-bit key sizes under full round considerations. These tests collectively span many well-known properties that any good cryptographic algorithm should satisfy. Column B denotes the number of binary sequences that are rejected as being random by a statistical test at the 1% significance level. Columns C and D denote the proportion of binary sequences that passed a statistical test at the 1% significance level, and its associated likelihood, respectively.
