Abstract

原本 PDF p.5

要旨

Space operations are increasingly important to the national and economic security of the United States. Commercial space’s contribution to the critical infrastructure is growing in both volume and diversity of services as illustrated by the increased use of commercial communications satellite (COMSAT) bandwidth, purchase of commercial imagery, and the hosting of government payloads on commercial satellites. The U.S. government recognizes and supports space resilience as illustrated by numerous space policies, executive orders, and the National Cyber Strategy. The space cyber-ecosystem is an inherently risky, high-cost, and often inaccessible environment consisting of distinct yet interdependent segments. This report applies the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to the ground segment of space operations with an emphasis on the command and control of satellite buses and payloads.

Executive Summary

原本 PDF p.10

エグゼクティブサマリー

As stated in the September 2018 United States National Cyber Strategy, the U.S. Government (USG) considers unfettered access and freedom to operate in space vital to the advancement of the security, economic prosperity, and scientific knowledge of the nation, and is concerned about the growing cyber-related threats to space assets and their supporting infrastructure. [NCS-2018] The USG issued Space Policy Directive 5 (SPD-5) in 2020 which establishes key cybersecurity principles to guide and serve as the foundation for America’s approach to the cyber protection of space systems. SPD-5 also fosters practices within the USG and commercial space operations that protect space assets and their supporting infrastructure from cyber threats. [SPD-5]

原本 PDF p.10

2018年9月の米国国家サイバー戦略（United States National Cyber Strategy）に述べられているとおり、米国政府（USG）は、宇宙への妨げのないアクセスと宇宙で活動する自由を、国の安全保障、経済的繁栄、科学知識の発展にとって不可欠と考えており、宇宙資産とそれを支えるインフラに対するサイバー関連の脅威の増大を懸念しています。[NCS-2018] USG は2020年に宇宙政策指令 5（SPD-5）を発出しました。これは、宇宙システムのサイバー防護に対する米国のアプローチを導き、その基礎となる主要なサイバーセキュリティ原則を定めたものです。SPD-5 はまた、宇宙資産とそれを支えるインフラをサイバー脅威から保護する慣行を、USG および商業宇宙運用において促進します。[SPD-5]

The intent of this document is to introduce the Cybersecurity Framework by applying it to create a Profile for the space sector’s ground segment. 3.1. リスクマネジメントの概要

Risk management is the ongoing process of identifying, assessing, and responding to risk as related to an organization’s mission objectives. To manage risk, organizations should understand any potential impact as well as the likelihood that an event will occur. An organization should also consider statutory and policy requirements that may influence or inform cybersecurity decisions.

原本 PDF p.16

リスクマネジメントとは、組織のミッション目標に関連するリスクを識別、評価し、それに対応する継続的なプロセスです。リスクを管理するには、組織は潜在的な影響と、イベントが発生する可能性の両方を理解すべきです。組織はまた、サイバーセキュリティに関する意思決定に影響を与え、またはその判断材料となり得る、法令やポリシーの要件も考慮すべきです。

The Profile provides a flexible approach for stakeholders to manage risks when interfacing with the satellite bus or payload regardless of the source of the risk, including natural events, malicious actions, and human activities that have unintended consequences. It also provides a starting point from which organizations can customize their risk management approach.

原本 PDF p.16

本プロファイルは、自然事象、悪意ある行為、意図しない結果をもたらす人間の活動など、リスクの発生源にかかわらず、ステークホルダーが衛星バスまたはペイロードとやり取りする際のリスクを管理するための柔軟なアプローチを提供します。また、組織が自らのリスクマネジメントのアプローチをカスタマイズするための出発点も提供します。

The Profile is intended to be used in conjunction with existing risk management processes to provide additional risk management considerations. Examples of cybersecurity risk management processes include International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 31000:2018, ISO/International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) 27005, NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-39 [NIST-SP800-39], and NASA Risk Management Handbook [NASA-SP-2011]. A list of additional resources is included in Appendix C.

RS.IM-2: 対応戦略が更新されます。

  • 識別、防御、検知の機能を見直し、必要に応じて更新することで、障害モードに対する予防措置を決定します。
  • 新たに特定された脆弱性を適時に緩和するため、必要に応じて、防御、監視、検知、対応、復旧の機能を改訂します。

4.5. 復旧機能

The Recover Function develops and implements the appropriate activities to maintain resilience and restore any capabilities or services that were impaired due to a cybersecurity event.
